## Introduction to ERAS Photo Background Requirements
As a medical residency applicant, you're likely aware of the importance of submitting a high-quality ERAS photo. Your photo is often the first impression you make on program directors and selection committees, so it's crucial to get it right. One aspect of your ERAS photo that's often overlooked is the background. In this article, we'll delve into the rules and examples of what makes an acceptable ERAS photo background.

## Understanding ERAS Photo Specifications
Before we dive into the background requirements, let's quickly review the overall ERAS photo specifications. According to the AAMC, your ERAS photo should meet the following criteria:
- Size: 2.5 x 3.5 inches
- Resolution: 150 DPI
- File format: JPEG
- File size: Under 150 KB

## ERAS Photo Background Rules
So, what are the rules for ERAS photo backgrounds? The AAMC specifies that the background should be:
- **Plain**: Avoid busy or distracting backgrounds that may take attention away from your face.
- **Light-colored**: A light-colored background is preferred, as it helps to create contrast with your face and makes your photo more visually appealing.
- **Solid**: The background should be a solid color, without any patterns or textures.

## Examples of Acceptable ERAS Photo Backgrounds
Here are some examples of acceptable ERAS photo backgrounds:
- A plain white or light gray wall
- A light-colored backdrop, such as a photography studio's standard backdrop
- A solid-colored paper or screen, such as a light blue or beige color

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
When it comes to ERAS photo backgrounds, there are some common mistakes to avoid:
- **Busy or distracting backgrounds**: Avoid using backgrounds with patterns, textures, or bright colors, as they can be distracting and take attention away from your face.
- **Dark or bold colors**: Dark or bold colors can make your face appear washed out or unflattering, so it's best to stick with light, neutral colors.
- **Inconsistent lighting**: Make sure the lighting in your photo is consistent and even, without any harsh shadows or bright spots.

## Tips for Taking a Great ERAS Photo
Here are some tips for taking a great ERAS photo:
- **Use a plain background**: As we discussed earlier, a plain background is essential for a professional-looking ERAS photo.
- **Dress professionally**: Wear professional attire, such as a white coat or business clothes, to present yourself in a professional light.
- **Smile and relax**: A natural, relaxed smile can make a big difference in your photo. Try to relax and be yourself, and avoid forced or awkward expressions.
- **Use good lighting**: Good lighting is essential for a high-quality photo. Natural light is always best, but if you're taking your photo indoors, try to use soft, even lighting.

## Concrete Steps to Resize Your ERAS Photo
Once you've taken your ERAS photo, you'll need to resize it to meet the AAMC's specifications. Here are the concrete steps to follow:
1. **Open your photo editing software**: You can use any photo editing software, such as Adobe Photoshop or a free online tool.
2. **Select the crop tool**: Choose the crop tool and select the 2.5 x 3.5 inch aspect ratio.
3. **Resize your photo**: Resize your photo to 150 DPI, making sure to maintain the aspect ratio.
4. **Save as a JPEG**: Save your photo as a JPEG file, making sure it's under 150 KB.
